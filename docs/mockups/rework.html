TROVERework mockup · v1

Four screens instead of thirteen. Home is a finder, not a dashboard: one search box, then only what needs you. Everything lives in one list (Stuff) and one map of the house (Places). Shopping turns a tick into stock. Real items and places come from your data; the blankets, chargers and jacket are examples from your message. Nothing here is built yet.
